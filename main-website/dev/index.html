Welcome

Welcome to the Tower Creation Guide for Onyx's Towers of Mayhem.
This comprehensive guide will walk you through how to create a quality tower for OToM, as well as our creation guidelines, tower types, and game mechanics.
Don't have Roblox Studio experience? Don't worry - we also go over some of the basics that are needed in Studio. Without further ado, let's get started!

Tower rules

Our tower creation guidelines differ quite a bit from EToH's in terms of leniency, but keeps most of its rules respected its rules for reference). Before creating a tower, always remember to check back here, as the tower creation guidelines may differ through the game's legacy and may change at any point of time.

Building

  • To prevent mix-ups, you can only use our dedicated tower kit as it has specific features that aren't included in EToH's. It's built from the ground up, so unfortunately converting an EToH submitted tower to an OToM compatible tower is not possible.
  • No free models, generative AI, content violating Roblox's ToS, or malicious assets.
  • For now, only CCO's (Custom Client Objects) can be used for Esteemed Builders.
  • Please keep the building format intact (will be posted later.)
  • The total floor count must be in coordination with the tower type's intended floor count. For more information, refer to the Tower types section.
  • A tower must have a frame OR something that vaguely resembles a frame.

Submissions

  • You may necropost your submission, but only if it has been at least a week and no one has reviewed your tower yet. Please note that necroposting repetitively will not make your tower be reviewed faster and may result in punishment.
  • Credit users that built at least 10% of the tower's gameplay in certain sections.
  • Joke towers will not be tolerated within the server's actual submission channel. However, you can submit those towers in our upcoming joke subrealm submission channel (will be posted soon.) Otherwise, don't post broken, old, or low-effort towers.
  • By submitting a tower, you agree that your tower can be removed if serious matters occur involving you within your role in EToH and there are multiple allegations or controversy.

A short rundown of what to include in your submission is your tower's name, place (must be made in Studio and publicly accessible), and the intended difficulty of the tower.

Name

Each tower has a unique identity; its name. But there are a few ground rules:

  • The tower name must start with "[Tower/Citadel/Steeple…] of" before the name. Cannot exceed 50 characters.
  • The tower name must not conflict with any other submitted towers in the game and connected games (excluding EToH!)
  • Make sure the tower name is in line with Roblox's Terms of Service: does not conflict with any controversial or political events or feed into a problematic issue in general. Your tower name may be filtered via Roblox's API and may not be used if it goes against it.

Prerequisites

You need a functional computer in order to create a tower because Roblox Studio is only available on PC. An old laptop that's been collecting dust in the corner? That probably works. An ancient iPhone SE? No. Don't even try it. WIP, subject to change

Tower place

  • Must provide a functional Noclip and Heal tool within StarterPack.
  • Tower music must be provided if possible. Make sure the music is at least copyright-free/free use OR you have permission to use songs from a certain artist. Consider using a tool like eProves to check if a song is copyright-free or not. If you're not sure - ask us.
  • Tower place must at least have a 16+ age restriction.

Tower types

Here you can find all of OToM's tower types. Some are exclusive to this game!

Bastion

Obviously, a bastion is a beheamoth. It's the tallest tower type we currently have available. Bastions usually consist of grueling (mostly purist) gameplay and are elusively known for their length (averaging around 3-6 hours long), as well as their harsh punishment. Bastions may have multiple basement/outside/dropper sections and are not counted as individual floors. The dimension of each floor ranges from 125x125 to 150x150. Individual floors can be extended up to 250 studs outwards for each direction. The difficulty of a bastion ranges between Remorseless and Terrifying. There can only be one bastion in each sector, serving as a finale. Completing this tower type currently yields a staggering 20 tower points and counts as one tower. Bastions can have between 50 and 100 floors and require a separate game/place connected to OToM with forced ULDM in order to be playable due to their extreme part count, lag, and loss of maintainablity if simply kept ingame. The win message is global.

Crypt

Crypts are our second-tallest tower type. While as not quite as long as a typical citadel (around 2-3 hours long), crypts could be comparable to a 'mini bastion' due to their similar dimensions. Crypts are intended to be secluded, so outside gameplay should be minimal. Crypts may have multiple basement/outside/dropper sections and are not counted as individual floors. The dimension of each floor ranges from 120x120 to 130x130. Individual floors can be extended up to 50 studs outwards for each direction. The difficulty of a crypt ranges between Challenging and Terrifying. There can be a maximum of three crypts in each sector and one in each area within that sector. Crypts are optional and are not required to complete each sector. Therefore, they cannot be included in area requirements. Completing this tower type currently yields ⌈5+|(f30)|⌉ tower points, maxing out to 6.5 tower points.

Citadel

Citadels are essentially a longer tower type. Usually, citadels take an hour or two to complete in full, but may be a tad bit longer due to long basement, outside, or dropper sections. Citadels may have some basement/outside/dropper sections and are typically not counted as floors, but must be used sparingly. The dimension of each floor ranges from 100x100 to 110x110. Individual floors can be extended up to 200 studs outwards for each direction. The difficulty of a citadel ranges between Hard and Terrifying; breaking EToH's 'no soul-crushing citadels' convention. There can only be one citadel in each area, and must be somewhat easily accessible depending on the difficulty of the citadel. Completing this tower type currently grants ⌈2+|(f12)|⌉ tower points, maxing out to 4 tower points. Citadels can have between 12 to 28 floors and the win message is global.

Tower

Towers. The baseline - the gold standard. Typically, towers can take as short as 10 minutes and as long as about an hour. Towers may have two brief basement sections (not rooms; individual sections) and two small dropper sections, but both must be used sparingly. Outside sections (counted as outside of the main frame) do not have a specific threshold. The dimension of each floor is same as EToH's standard - being only 100x100. The difficulty of a tower can range between Easy and Arduous . Towers only give a singular tower point, and there are no exceptions to this rule. Towers have between 10 floors and may have 9 floors under special conditions. The win message is global for soul-crushing towers.

Steeple

Steeples are smaller towers. The length of a steeple depends on its gameplay, but has the same length range as a standard tower. Steeples may have one short basement section and one small dropper section. The dimension of each floor varies between 80x80 and 100x100. The difficulty of a steeple can range between Easy and Arduous. Steeples give 12 tower point. Steeples have between 4 and 8 floors. Like towers, soul-crushing win messages are global.
